Homey Palettes

Warm brown/tan directions without orange accents. These can be applied to any of the left-chat/right-resume layouts below.

Walnut + Sage

Cozy, grounded, and still technical. The green accent keeps it support/ops instead of coffee-shop.

walnut / espresso / oat / sage / ivory

Oat + Ink

Bright enough for recruiters, warmer than the current blue-white look, with a serious left chat panel.

oat / ink / cream / blue-gray / linen line

Mushroom + Spruce

Earthy and calm. Good if you want homey without beige monotony or orange warmth.

mushroom / spruce / stone / herb / parchment

Linen + Slate

The safest professional option: homey paper tones, cool slate structure, no orange energy.

linen / slate / cream / cool gray / white card

Coffee + Blue

Still warm and personal, but the blue accent gives it a technical/admin feel instead of rustic.

coffee / charcoal / warm paper / dusty blue / cream

Taupe + Plum

Soft, homey, and a little distinctive. The muted plum gives personality without going flashy.

taupe / deep plum / clay-gray / muted plum / milk

Blush + Slate

Homey but cooler and more modern: blush paper, slate structure, pale sage cards, and deep navy depth.

blush / slate / pale sage / mauve-gray / navy
